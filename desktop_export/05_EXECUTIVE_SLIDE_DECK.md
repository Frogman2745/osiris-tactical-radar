# OSIRIS — 12-Slide Executive Deck

## Slide 1 — Title
OSIRIS: Wildfire Operations Simulation and DAG Orchestrator. State that every operational value is synthetic.

## Slide 2 — Problem
Status views and data-workflow controls are often separated.

## Slide 3 — Product concept
One interface combines a Canvas map, asset dossiers, predictive views, pipeline dependencies, collaboration, and documentation.

## Slide 4 — Tactical-map engineering
Canvas 2D rendering, geodetic projection, vector symbology, zoom/pan, hover hit testing, and locked target dossiers.

## Slide 5 — DAG orchestration
Configurable stages, dependencies, retries, parallel flags, notes, and JSON export.

## Slide 6 — Predictive views
Five scenario views cover pits, stock, fleet, turnaround, and sorties.

## Slide 7 — Reliability exercises
Failure injection demonstrates latency, schema drift, connection loss, resource pressure, and recovery paths.

## Slide 8 — Collaboration
The Express/WebSocket server supports a local multi-client demonstration and versioned report editing.

## Slide 9 — Provenance correction
Show the demo badge, non-routable example endpoint, synthetic labels, and scenario-parameter disclosure.

## Slide 10 — Engineering evidence
Show current test, TypeScript, and build results with the commit SHA.

## Slide 11 — Limitations
No live agency feed, operational authorization, safety-of-flight use, or guaranteed production-scale performance.

## Slide 12 — Portfolio takeaway
OSIRIS demonstrates frontend architecture, complex state visualization, local real-time collaboration, testing, documentation, and responsible provenance.
